BAROWEATHER - GOALS
===================

PRIMARY
-------
1. Show barometric pressure prominently with low/very-high/incoming warnings in
   a red banner at the top of the screen.
2. Be a complete, attractive weather app: current conditions, hourly, 7-day,
   details, settings, saved places.
3. Work on small phones and tablets, Android 5.0+.
4. Put cross-platform logic in Rust; use C++ only if Rust cannot bridge cleanly.
5. Keep the Android APK dependency-free and sideloadable with local SDK tools.

PRESSURE
--------
6. Use sea-level pressure from Open-Meteo, with a keyless MET Norway forecast
   fallback; show surface pressure as secondary only.
7. Detect current low (<1005 hPa default), current very high (>1030 hPa), and
   significant 3 h / 12 h falls or rises.
8. Make thresholds and trend sensitivity configurable.
9. Warn before low pressure arrives using the next-12-hour forecast.
10. Include a clear, visible non-medical-advice disclaimer.

GUI
---
11. Red top banner with icon, title, value, window, and WHY? button.
12. Expanded pressure card with gauge, trend chips and 24 h sparkline.
13. Hero current-conditions card, 24 h hourly strip, 7-day forecast, details.
14. Custom canvas weather icons and gauges; no image assets.
15. Phone portrait, phone landscape, tablet portrait, tablet landscape layouts.
16. Dark and light themes, high contrast, system font scaling, TalkBack labels.

DATA / PLATFORM
---------------
17. Open-Meteo forecast + geocoding over HTTPS, no API key; MET Norway
    Locationforecast as a keyless forecast-pressure fallback only.
18. Location permission with manual city fallback.
19. Cache last forecast for offline viewing with timestamp.
20. Java fallback when the Rust native library is unavailable.

ADDED IN v0.4
-------------
- Plain-language pressure story and private symptom log.
- Quiet incoming-pressure notifications and a cached home-screen widget.
- Multiple saved places, colour-blind-safe chart patterns and reduce motion.
- PNG/CSV pressure history export via the platform share sheet.

ADDED IN v0.4.1
---------------
- Rain chance percentage and expected millimetres in forecast rows/details.
- 110% text option, single-line large temperature, and blue/navy/matrix themes.

ADDED IN v0.4.2
---------------
- Dedicated starred-favorites page with toolbar shortcut, search/current add, open and remove.

ADDED IN v0.4.3
---------------
- Keyless MET Norway forecast-pressure fallback behind Open-Meteo, with provider
  attribution and no secret key in the APK.
- Provider-aware cache, identifying User-Agent and forecast-only scope; coarser
  samples are interpolated hourly, but past trend, long history and geocoding
  stay with Open-Meteo.

ADDED IN v0.4.4
---------------
- 16 KB ELF and APK alignment for the native core so the app loads on Android 15
  tablets with 16 KB memory pages, such as the Lenovo Idea Tab Pro (TB373FU).
- Java fallback retained for unsupported ABIs or any native-load failure.

OUT OF SCOPE FOR v0.1
---------------------
- Medical diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Ads, analytics, billing, Play Services.
- Weather radar maps and severe-weather government alert feeds.
- Wear OS / automotive builds.
